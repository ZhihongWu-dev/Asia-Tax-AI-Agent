Important

This calculator serves as a guide for the Payer to find out the applicable Withholding Tax Rate as specified in the Income Tax Act or Avoidance of Double Taxation Agreement (DTA) entered into by Singapore.

It is the responsibility of the Payer:-

a)
To ascertain the business structure and tax residency of the non-resident so that the correct tax rate can be determined.
b)
To comply with the requirements on claiming of Double Taxation Relief (DTR), if applicable.
c)
To withhold, file and pay the correct amount of withholding tax by the due date.

Failure to do so, may result in recovery of tax from the Payer and/or imposition of late payment penalties.
