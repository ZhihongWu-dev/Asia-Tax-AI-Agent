Rule 7(1)(

)
Conditions for liquidation or winding up
Part 1
Transferor entity
Liquidation or winding up of transferor entity
Form of association
between transferor entity
and transferee entity before
liquidation or winding up
Conditions for liquidation or winding up
Association within the meaning of rule 3(1)(
b
)(i)
(
a
)
Where the transferor entity was a holding entity of the transferee entity —
(i)
the transferor entity was not, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, the ultimate holding entity of the transferee entity; and
(ii)
the transferee entity remains associated, for a period of 2 years from the date of execution of the instrument referred to in rule 4(
a
), with —
(A)
the permitted entity that was, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, the immediate holding entity of the transferor entity; and
(B)
any permitted entity that was, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, both —
(BA)
a holding entity of the transferee entity; and
(BB)
an entity in relation to which the transferor entity was a holding entity.
(
b
)
Where the transferee entity was a holding entity of the transferor entity, the transferee entity remains associated, for a period of 2 years from the date of execution of the instrument referred to in rule 4(
a
), with the permitted entity that was, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, the immediate holding entity of the transferee entity.
Association within the meaning of rule 3(1)(
b
)(ii)
The transferee entity remains associated, for a period of 2 years from the date of execution of the instrument referred to in rule 4(
a
), with any permitted entity (other than the transferor entity) that, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, was the immediate holding entity of the transferor entity.

Conditions for liquidation or winding up
Association within the meaning of rule 3(1)(
b
)(i)
(
a
)
Where the transferee entity was a holding entity of the transferor entity —
(i)
the transferee entity was not, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, the ultimate holding entity of the transferor entity; and
(ii)
the transferor entity remains associated, for a period of 2 years from the date of execution of the instrument referred to in rule 4(
a
), with the permitted entity (other than the transferee entity) that was, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, the immediate holding entity of the transferee entity.
(
b
)
Where the transferor entity was a holding entity of the transferee entity, the transferor entity remains associated, for a period of 2 years from the date of execution of the instrument referred to in rule 4(
a
) with the permitted entity that was, immediately prior to the date of liquidation, both —
(i)
a holding entity of the transferee entity; and
(ii)
a permitted entity in relation to which the transferor entity was a holding entity.
Association within the meaning of rule 3(1)(
b
)(ii)
The transferor entity remains associated, for a period of 2 years from the date of execution of the instrument referred to in rule 4(
a
), with any permitted entity (other than the transferee entity) that, immediately prior to the date of liquidation or winding up, was the immediate holding entity of the transferee entity.
