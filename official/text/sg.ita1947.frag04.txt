14.
—(1) For the purpose of ascertaining the income of any person for any period from any source chargeable with tax under this Act (called in this Part the income), there are to be deducted all outgoings and expenses wholly and exclusively incurred during that period by that person in the production of the income, including —
(
a
)
except as provided in this section —
(i)

(ii)

(

(

(i)
any doubtful debt;
(ii)
any other debt for which provisions have been made for impairment losses or expected credit losses (called in this paragraph provisioned debt),

(iii)
